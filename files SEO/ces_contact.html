Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

?Is CES registered for CSR contributions under Companies Act 2013?
Yes. CES is registered on the MCA CSR portal with CSR-1 number CSR00095049. All contributions are valid under Schedule VII of the Companies Act 2013. We provide utilisation certificates and impact reports.
?Is my donation tax exempt under 80G?
Yes. CES holds a valid 80G certificate (AAATC3478QF20241) under the Income Tax Act. Individual and corporate donors receive official receipts within 24 hours for claiming deductions.
?Can we visit the campus before deciding to partner?
Absolutely. We welcome site visits for CSR teams, foundation trustees, and individual donors. Contact us to schedule a guided visit to our Waigaon campus and meet students and faculty.
?What is the minimum contribution amount?
There is no minimum. ₹50,000 furnishes one hostel room for a girl student. ₹5,000 covers one month's hostel expenses for a student. Every amount has a named, specific use.
?Do you accept international donations?
Currently we receive contributions from Indian residents and companies. For international contributions, please contact us to discuss the applicable process. We are exploring FCRA registration.
?How do you report on utilisation of funds?
We provide formal utilisation certificates, photographic documentation, and student impact stories for every significant contribution. Annual reports and audited statements are available on request.
